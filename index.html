Our Heritage

Where Classical French Pastry Meets Modern Design

Founded in 2011 by Master Pâtissier Antoine Laurent, BakeStudio began with a singular obsession: to eliminate compromise in pastry. We reject artificial flavor enhancers, hydrogenated shortenings, and frozen premixes.

Today, our state-of-the-art studio bridges the gap between artisanal culinary artistry and digital convenience. With our interactive cake builder and enterprise distribution fleet, we empower clients to design and receive bespoke confections with absolute peace of mind.

Inquiries & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about custom bookings, ingredient standards, and delivery protocols.

Standard designer cakes can be booked through our interactive builder with as little as 24 to 48 hours notice. For elaborate multi-tier wedding cakes requiring sugar flower sculpting and structural armature, we recommend reserving your delivery slot 2 to 4 weeks in advance.

We operate a physically isolated eggless and plant-based baking bay with dedicated stone ovens, stand mixers, and spatulas. We use pure European flax and plant-derived emulsifiers to achieve a tender, moist sponge without any egg traces.

Our proprietary fleet of zero-tilt delivery vans maintains an interior temperature of 4°C to 6°C regardless of outside summer weather. We service all addresses within a 45 km radius of our central atelier with real-time SMS driver tracking.

Cafés, hotels, and corporate offices can register for an enterprise account via our Enterprise Hub. We provide itemized GST tax invoices, PO matching, volume discount tiers (up to 35% off retail), and Net-30 credit terms.
