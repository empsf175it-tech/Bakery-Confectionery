# Single Page Website Architecture

## Section Breakdown (In Exact Required Sequence)

1. **`#home` — Hero & Brand Showcase**
   - Brand headline with animated badge.
   - 4 Animated floating pastry visuals with keyframe floating animations.
   - Trust statistics counters (18,500+ celebrations, 480+ B2B partners, 99.8% on-time delivery).
   - Key features strip with FSSAI hygiene credentials.
   - Infinite marquee flavor ribbon.

2. **`#book-now` — The Interactive Feature Hub**
   - **Interactive Cake Customizer:** 2D/3D dynamic preview showing shape, tier heights, frosting color swatches, toppings, and piped script font inscription.
   - **Instant Pricing Calculator:** Live transparent breakdown of base price, size multiplier, toppings, and delivery fee with animated counter. Bulk discount stepper (10% to 25% off).
   - **Delivery Slot Picker:** Interactive calendar with day blocking, time-slot grid with availability badges, pickup vs delivery toggle, and postal/pincode checker.
   - **5-Step Booking Wizard:** Step indicator progress bar, validation with error tooltips, order summary card, and unique Order ID generation (`AMR-2026-XXXX`).

3. **`#services` — Culinary Spectrum (9 Cards)**
   - 9 cards with **3D Tilt effect** on all cards.
   - Icons and text are strictly **CENTER ALIGNED** in all desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints.
   - Bakery-related micro-animations on cards (Whisk rotation, Rising dough bounce, Steam rising, Frosting drip reveal, Gold shimmer, Sprinkles fall, Cake slice float).

4. **`#enterprise` — Enterprise Hub**
   - Solutions for luxury hotels, cafés, restaurants, and corporate offices.
   - Feature comparison matrix table.
   - 3 Partnership tier cards with 3D tilt and centered text.
   - Wholesale credit terms and GST invoicing specifications.

5. **`#gallery` — Filterable Portfolio & Accessible Lightbox**
   - 10 unique high-resolution local WebP images.
   - Filter categories: All, Designer Cakes, Artisan Pastries, Macarons & Cookies, Fine Chocolates, Wedding & Grand Tiers.
   - Accessible Lightbox modal with zoom and "Order This Design" direct action.

6. **`#process` — Process Timeline**
   - 5-step journey: Choose → Formulate → Bake → Decorate → Climate Delivery.

7. **`#testimonials` — Customer Praise Slider**
   - Touch-enabled carousel with autoplay, pause on hover, star ratings, and avatars.

8. **`#about` — Story, Master Chefs & Hygiene Badges**
   - Atelier heritage and sourcing credentials.
   - 3 Master Chef profile cards.
   - FSSAI Grade A+, ISO 22000, and HACCP certifications.

9. **`#faq` — Accessible Accordion**
   - Keyboard navigable FAQ items with ARIA expanded states.

10. **`#newsletter` — The Pâtisserie Society**
    - Mailchimp / ConvertKit compatible subscription form with 15% discount perk.

11. **`#contact` — Contact & Tasting Salon**
    - Formspree / Netlify Forms compatible form with floating validation.
    - Operating hours, studio map embed, and direct WhatsApp hotline.

12. **`footer` — Studio Footer**
    - Social links, quick navigation, legal, and payment method badges.
